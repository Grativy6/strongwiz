# Calibration 002 — Strongwiz v2 adaptive Kevin Speak campaign

Final status: **PARTIAL**. The owner requested a resource pause during Stage 4.
The final official state was `NOT_FINISHED` at 1 of 7 levels, and completion was
not genuinely observed. See [`002-result.md`](002-result.md) and the public
receipt set under [`receipts/002/`](receipts/002/).

Calibration 002 is a non-contest, local-public calibration on the exact public
`ls20-9607627b` artifact used by Calibration 001. Official
`arcengine.GameState.WIN` is the sole completion authority.

The campaign keeps Calibration 001's aggregate ceiling but divides it into four
fresh labs: 30, 60, 90, and 300 minutes. Their action/reset/call allocations are
128/4/132, 256/8/264, 384/12/396, and 1280/40/1320. Each lab begins with a new
seed-0 `Arcade.make` instance. It may receive only a target-bound, receipt-backed
learned stack from the preceding sealed stage.

Allowed transfer is deliberately narrow: reviewed and adopted Kevin Speak
translations and concise retained-mechanic statements. Frames, raw traces,
action sequences, domain state, private reasoning, and authority do not cross.
The source model may recommend the next codebook, but a separate clean-room
review and explicit adoption decision control successor use. Rejected or
deferred symbols remain readable evidence and inactive.

This is one adaptive campaign, not four independent trials. Comparison with
Calibration 001 is historical and descriptive; it does not identify a causal
effect or establish generalization.
