## What v3 changes from v2

Calibration 002 remains `PARTIAL`: the official state was `NOT_FINISHED` at
1/7 levels after 699 non-reset actions, 12 resets, and 711 total environment
calls. Its Kevin Speak ledger stayed blank because the action operator also had
to notice and author shorthand. V3 therefore adds a separately identified
representation scribe and tests it against an otherwise matched no-scribe arm.

The prepared campaign also:

- keeps one live environment lineage across bookkeeping stages instead of
  automatically paying the level-reacquisition tax;
- makes evidence yield, not elapsed time alone, the stage boundary;
- preserves v2's smaller reference-normalized ledgers and streaming paths;
- imports prior mechanics only through an exact, target-bound adaptive-successor
  packet, never as fresh generalization evidence;
- binds PAL v2.3 work projections, complete checkpoint capsules, non-work
  coordinate revalidation, immutable grant epochs, transport breaks, and
  administrative heartbeat stutter;
- journals scribe requests and frozen drafts before cross-account mutation;
  and
- uses injective session-bound Kevin entry/evaluation identities and refuses
  conflicting semantic recommendation IDs.

These changes are hypotheses and integrity mechanisms. They have not yet shown
that a scribe improves compression, model reasoning, action efficiency, or ARC
progress.

## Residuals and next boundary

The scribe's closed request view omits held-out payloads, but the callable
driver remains trusted in-process code rather than a confidentiality sandbox.
Free-text source truth and forbidden-content classification remain upstream
responsibilities. Typed carry fields establish pinned provenance structure,
not the truth or sufficiency of the source claim. PAL digest relations establish
declared identity, not independent truth about their referents.

Calibration 003 is prepared but deliberately **not run and not yet
preregistered against the frozen toolbelt**. The next step is to bind an exact
operator/runtime, domain/game version, matched budgets, campaign roots, and
presentation mode in a successor preregistration. Only a separate owner
instruction should then create the fresh labs or contact the public ARC
environment.

Hearthline component was used, and no competition entry, credential, account,
